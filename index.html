Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. You get 15 free questions every day in all nine subjects, with explanations. Advanced Access adds longer quizzes with no daily limit, from ₦1,000 a month.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start. You only enter a license key if you buy Advanced Access.

How accurate are the questions?

The AI writes questions to follow the exam syllabus, but it can make mistakes. If a question looks wrong, check it with your teacher and tell us on WhatsApp so we can fix it.

Is ExamPrep AI part of JAMB, WAEC or NECO?

No. ExamPrep AI is an independent practice tool made by ApexDynamics Solutions. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by JAMB, WAEC or NECO.

How do I get Advanced Access?

Choose the 1-month plan or the 3-month exam-season pass and pay. Use the email you want the key tied to. We send the license key to you within a few hours. Open the app, enter that email and key in the Advanced Access panel, and select Verify key.

Can my school use it?

Yes. Start with a free pilot for one class. Paid licences are priced per term by the number of students. There is no teacher dashboard or per-student tracking yet, so teachers can ask students to share their scores in a class group.

Does it work offline?

No. You need an internet connection to generate questions.
